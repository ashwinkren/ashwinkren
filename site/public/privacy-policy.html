Smart Scroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What Smart Scroll does

Smart Scroll is a Chrome extension that tracks Instagram/TikTok Reels viewing behavior locally: dwell time, scroll patterns, interactions, and optional AI classification.

Data storage

All analytics stay on your device in Chrome local storage. We do not operate a server that collects your viewing history.

Optional Anthropic API (BYOK)

If you add your own API key, reel frames/captions are sent to Anthropic for classification. You pay Anthropic directly. We never receive or store your key on any server.

Feed Trainer

When you press Start, the extension scrolls Reels automatically to train your feed toward categories you choose. Runs only in your browser.

Exports

CSV and JSON exports are saved to your computer only when you click Export. We do not receive export files.
